# Reflection

## The 3 biggest things I learned

1. **How to test a model fairly.** In Week 8 I learned train/test splits, cross-validation and GridSearchCV, and in Week 9 and this project I saw that time series need a time-based split, because a random split lets the model see the future. A model is only useful if it is tested on data it has not seen.

2. **Association is not causation.** In Week 6 the hypothesis tests (t-test and chi-square) showed me that a small p-value tells us about evidence against the null hypothesis, not about why something happens. I now say "associated with" and I list limitations next to my results.

3. **The business question and the data matter as much as the model.** In the Week 11 mini project, and again in this capstone, most of the value came from cleaning the data, exploring it and asking a clear question. The model comparison showed that a fancier model does not help much when the data does not contain the main drivers (quantity, discount and price).

## What I want to keep improving

1. **Feature engineering and model tuning on richer data**, for example predicting profit and using more advanced forecasting models.
2. **Communicating results**: building simple dashboards and explaining technical results clearly in a short presentation.
